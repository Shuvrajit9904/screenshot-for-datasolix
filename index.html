Agentic data conversion · Oracle Fusion

Month-long conversions, run in hours.

Connect your source systems, including EBS, JDE, and more. Particle’s AI agents run the conversion to Oracle Fusion autonomously, from extraction to load. Your team reviews exceptions and approves key decisions.

Data conversion holds up implementations. Teams spend weeks extracting records, fixing inconsistent data, building mappings, and chasing failed loads. Each correction creates another round of spreadsheets, checks, and approvals.

01 / CLEAN

Clean data.
Prepared autonomously.

Particle extracts records, resolves duplicates, fills missing values from source evidence, validates addresses, and builds Fusion mappings. Remaining source issues come with the exact record and field to update.

02 / VERIFY

A tested load.
Fewer surprises.

An automated mock load catches rejects, missing dependencies, and target setup gaps before production. Each blocker comes with a diagnosis, a proposed fix, and the number of records it could recover.

03 / RECONCILE

Every row reconciled.
Every change explained.

Automatic reconciliation accounts for merges, exclusions, and rejects. The audit trail captures every action and its evidence. Your team reviews exceptions and approves key decisions before work continues.
